A tiny notch

Now playing with synced lyrics, a file shelf, clipboard history, notifications and friends, all in a small notch at the top of your screen. With a fox.

Download for Windows
Latest version · Windows 10 & 11 · free and open source

Everything at a glance

Hover the notch to open it. Move away and it tucks itself back in.

Music & lyrics

Whatever is playing (Spotify, browsers, any media app) with controls and lyrics that slide in line by line.

Friends

Add friends with a code like FUZ-A4G7B9. See who's online and what they play, poke them, or listen along.

Shelf & clipboard

Drop files on the notch to keep them handy, and get back anything you copied, text or images.

Notifications & HUDs

Messages, volume, Caps Lock and audio device changes show up right in the notch.

Install

No installer: Fuzzie is a single file.

  1. Download Fuzzie.exe and put it wherever you like, for example in a "Fuzzie" folder in Documents.
  2. Run it. Windows may say it protected your PC because the app is new: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. That's it. Fuzzie starts with Windows and updates itself. Open Settings from the gear in the notch or the tray icon.
